## 2026-10-10 - CHANGELOG entries for domain terminology qualification
**Learning:** PRs that update app summary/description metadata (e.g. Lexicon domain terminology qualification) require an entry under `## [Unreleased]` in `CHANGELOG.md`.
**Action:** When auditing unreleased changes, check `git log <last-release-tag>..HEAD` and verify metadata/terminology changes are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
